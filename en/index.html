Tax rule applied

  • Tax relief of 60% of the donated amount (article 238 bis of the French Tax Code), 40% for the part of donations above €2M in the year, except for organisations helping people in difficulty, which stay at 60%.
  • Cap: €20,000 of donations a year, or 0.5% of revenue excl. VAT if that amount is higher. The part above the cap is carried forward over the next five financial years.
  • In-kind donation valued at stock cost price: here the item's stock value in its condition (40% of the selling price, then 75% in good condition and 30% in poor condition).
  • The recipient charity must be eligible and issue a tax receipt (Cerfa form no. 16216). Financial year assumed to match the calendar year.
  • Revenue estimated at €215,000 excl. VAT: €46,405 of returns over 12 months divided by an 18% return rate in online fashion (Shipup by ZigZag and Sapio Research, May 2026), i.e. about €258,000 incl. VAT. The cap therefore stays at €20,000.

Source: French Ministry of Culture, Le régime fiscal général du mécénat d'entreprise (general tax regime for corporate giving), accessed on 17/11/2026.

Regulatory context

The destruction ban in the ESPR regulation (article 25) applies to large companies since 19/07/2026 and to medium-sized ones from 19/07/2030. Small and micro-businesses are exempt. Our fictitious e-retailer (estimated revenue €215,000) is a micro-business: setting is No. The French AGEC law (alert J01) applies whatever the size.

Data used

Method

Data
Fully synthetic catalogue and returns batch, from the Stock and Inbound Customer Returns tabs of the English Google Sheet Group-5_Data-Sheet-EN (100 references and 500 returns). No company data. The French version of the tool reads the French original Sheet, which a script feeds with 40 fictitious returns on the 1st of each month.
Values
Customer value = catalogue selling price. Stock value = 40% of the selling price, then 75% and 30% of that value for good and poor condition; each condition has its own reference (e.g. 128419, 128419B, 128419C).
Decision logic
The first rule matching the category, the product type (taken from the item name), the condition and, if set, the customer value threshold gives the route, in increasing order of priority. Default rules cover any catalogue item whose type is not recognised, and never choose destruction. An item whose reference does not exist in the tool is rejected at receiving, unless the operator first creates the reference (category, type, price): the route is then calculated as for a catalogue item.
Donations
Items sent to donation are grouped by month and year based on the return date. Tax relief = rate (60%) × stock value donated, up to €20,000 or 0.5% of revenue excl. VAT.
Calculations
Cost = quantity × handling cost of the route. Value recovered = quantity × selling price × percentage recovered. Net result = value recovered − cost.
Compliance
Alerts from the Legal tab: AGEC law, EU ESPR regulation and Delegated Regulation 2026/296, product safety, French Consumer Code, French Tax Code. ESPR alerts are only shown for a large company. With an estimated revenue of €215,000, our e-retailer is a micro-business: the setting is No by default and can be changed in Rules & parameters.
Customer status
Credited or not credited: shown for information, it does not decide the item's physical route (alert J14).
AI use
Tool built with Claude (Anthropic), which also structured the Google Sheet, suggested texts and translated this English version. Returns batch built by Jade with Claude. ChatGPT (OpenAI) for parameters and legal, Gemini (Google) for rules and review. The rules, thresholds, ethical choices and reading of the law are the team's own, checked on 15 edge cases, 8 returns recalculated by hand and the official texts. The tool itself uses no AI: everything is calculated in the browser.
Limits
Parameters sourced from public references (France logistics benchmark 2026, studies on the resale of returns 2026), except the refurbishment cleaning cost, which is still a team assumption. No “sealed packaging” information for underwear. Changes made here stay in your browser. The tool reads the English Google Sheet (published read-only) each time it opens; if it cannot be reached, it uses the embedded copy.
